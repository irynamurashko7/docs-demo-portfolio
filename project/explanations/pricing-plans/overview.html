Pricing Plans

A Pricing Plan is an entity that contains information about the billing cycle period (for a subscription Offering) and a price calculation rule.

The pricing plan assigned to an Offering defines its price based on the configured billing cycle, price calculation rule, and designated values.

Each Offering distributed via the Core Platform must have at least one pricing plan.

For example, the Phone Service Partner may have the following setup:

  • Residential Phone Line Offering that has the following Pricing Plans providing the basic features:

    • Small with a limited amount of the basic features

    • Large with the basic features provided in larger or unlimited amounts

  • Business Phone Line Offering that has the following Pricing Plans providing the advanced features:

    • Business with the minimum number of advanced features

    • Enterprise with a larger number of advanced features

Types of Pricing Plans

Pricing Plans are divided into standalone (regular) and add-on pricing plans.

An Add-on Pricing Plan is a pricing plan that maintains parent-child relations with another pricing plan. It is used to extend the functions of existing Offerings with additional paid features, such as call recording, voicemail transcription, additional phone lines, etc.

An Add-on Pricing Plan is a specific pricing plan that customers can purchase only with particular pricing plans or for an existing Customer Item associated with one of these pricing plans. Such pricing plans are called Parent Pricing Plans, and such a Customer Item is called a Parent Customer Item.

A regular pricing plan becomes a Parent after one or several Add-on Pricing Plans are assigned to it.

When assigning an Add-on Pricing Plan to a Parent, a relation is created between them. As a result, the Add-on becomes available for purchase together with this Parent Pricing Plan or for an existing Parent Customer Item.

Configurations

Configurations are settings that are defined within the Pricing Plan during its creation or update. They include:

Billing Type

Pricing plans can use one of the following billing types:

  • single-purchase - used for single-purchase Offerings.

  • prepaid - used for subscription Offerings.

  • usage-based - used for usage-based Offerings.

Billing Cycle

A Billing Cycle is the time between two consecutive billing invoices for subscription Offerings. Billing cycles can be monthly (1 month - 11 months) or yearly (1 year - 5 years).

At the end of the billing cycle, the customer must pay the renewal price to continue using the subscription.

The default billing cycle is defined when creating the Pricing Plan. Based on this data, the Core Platform automatically creates a Pricing Plan Duration entity. The default Billing Cycle and Pricing Plan Duration can be updated later.

All other billing cycles can be added via separate Pricing Plan Durations.

Metadata

Metadata can be used to add additional details to the pricing plan that do not participate in the price calculation.

For example, the Partner needs to know how many phone lines the user adds to their account when configuring the associated Customer Item. This value does not change via API and does not participate in the price calculation rule. So, to retrieve it, they can add a variable for this information to the pricing plan metadata and then check it with other details of the pricing plan.

Purchase Settings

Purchase Settings is a configuration that defines whether any additional actions are required during purchase. PreAuthorizationRequired setting determines whether a pre-authorization is required when customers purchase the product.

Pre-authorization is a process of verifying the customer by charging and immediately refunding a small nominal amount (e.g., $0.05 or its equivalent in the customer’s currency) to their selected payment method.

Pre-authorization occurs if the Partner enables this option and the final total of the order is $0.00 (for example, for trial or usage-based subscriptions). This process helps to prevent fraudulent purchases by validating the customer’s payment method.

Relations

Relations are the settings for the Pricing Plan that are added using separate entities that must be created and assigned to one or more Pricing Plans within a Partner according to the instructions in the How-To Guides section of this documentation. They include:

Durations

One pricing plan can have several assigned durations. The duration represents the time interval between the start and end of a billing cycle. It also allows configuring trial and grace periods, the allowed billing cycle count, and adding a billing strategy.

Please check the Pricing Plan Durations document for more information.

Price Calculation Rule

One of the key aspects of the pricing plan is a Price Calculation Rule. It is used to calculate the item price based on the pricing plan parameters that can be passed through the API.

Due to the shared configurations approach, it is possible to assign an existing price calculation rule to a pricing plan or create a new one.

Please check the Price Calculation Rules document for more information.

Parameters

Parameters are global. This means that a parameter can be used by one or more price calculation rules within your Partner entity.

Each parameter has a default value. However, it is possible to override its default value and set a new one for various entities (Partner, Offering, Pricing Plan, Pricing Plan Duration, Price Calculation Rule).

Please check the Parameters document for more information on how parameters work.
